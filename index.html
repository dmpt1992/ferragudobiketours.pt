Ask us anything ↗
Do I book the self-guided tour separately? +

No. Reserve your bike on the Flow Ferragudo website. When you collect it, tell us which route interests you and we'll help you get set up.

How do I follow the route? +

We help set up the selected GPS route on your phone. A phone holder comes with your bike rental so you can follow the route while riding independently.

Can you organise a private guided ride? +

Private guided experiences are available by request on selected routes. Tell us your group size, dates and riding level, and we'll confirm whether a guide is available.

Which bike is best for me? +

For relaxing coastal rides, an e-bike is a great choice. MTB and e-MTB suit mixed terrain; gravel and road bikes are good for longer routes. We can help you decide.

Can we start from our accommodation? +

Bike delivery can be arranged in selected nearby areas. For some self-guided routes we can help adjust the starting point. Contact us first to confirm delivery and route suitability.
